# Findings from a synthetic scenario

- Affected-listing rate changes from 9.62% in May to 6.37% in October (-3.25 percentage points). The generator deliberately lowers failure probabilities; this does not prove an intervention worked.
- Electronics has the highest six-month affected-check rate (13.59%). In a real operation, investigate error types and supplier inputs before assigning more reviewers.
- At October month-end there are 1,681 open issue tickets affecting 1,452 distinct listings. Prioritise severity, then age; do not confuse tickets with products.

All 10,000 listings are checked once each month. This full-coverage assumption is unusually clean; production analysis must account for unchecked listings and changing coverage. Current export attributes are a separate illustrative dataset and cannot reconstruct historical issues. No revenue, customers, or real employer data are used.
